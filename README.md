What It Finds

Deal Hunter was built around five deal categories that most buyers never even think to search for:

**Assumable Mortgages**
Some sellers have existing government-backed loans that can legally be transferred to a new buyer — at the seller's original interest rate. If that rate is from 2020 or 2021, you could be stepping into a 2–3% mortgage in a 7% market. These deals exist. This tool finds them.

**Payment Takeover**
A structure where you take over the seller's existing mortgage payments and the property transfers to you — without originating a new loan. Rarely advertised. Almost never found by accident.

**Owner / Seller Financing**
The seller acts as the bank. No lender approval, no origination fees, no 30-day closing timeline. Terms are negotiated directly. These deals show up in listing descriptions if you know what to look for — NJ Deal Hunter knows.

**Rent-to-Own / Lease Option**
Move in now, lock in a purchase price, buy when you're ready. Ideal if you need time to build credit or savings while still securing a home at today's price.

**Distressed & Motivated Sellers**
Pre-foreclosure, estate sales, as-is listings, aggressive price reductions. Sellers in these situations are almost always open to creative terms because they need to move the property fast.

How to Use It

**1. Open the app.**
No account. No signup. No download. It works in your browser on any device.

**2. Set your location.**
Type your city, county, or ZIP code. Leave it broad or get hyper-specific — your call.

**3. Choose your strictness level.**
This controls how tightly the results are filtered to active listings only:
- **Balanced** — Best for most people. Filters out sold and pending listings.
- **Strict** — Maximum filtering. Cleanest results. Recommended if you've been burned by stale listings before.
- **Wide** — More results, some may be older. Use when you want volume.

> One thing to know: every result is guaranteed to be a property listing page — not an article, not a blog post, not sold history. Whether it's still actively for sale depends on your strictness setting. The app tells you exactly what's guaranteed on every search card.

**4. Set your budget.**
Enter your max price, min price, max monthly payment, and max down payment — any or all of them. Your monthly budget can be injected directly into searches to match listings that mention payment amounts in their description.

**5. Pick your deal types.**
Toggle on or off any combination of the five deal categories. Focus only on what applies to your situation.

**6. Pick your sources.**
Choose which platforms to search. All are on by default. Turn off ones you've already checked today.

**7. Add keywords (optional).**
Must-include words, words to exclude, or a custom phrase to narrow your results further.

**8. Open your search cards.**
Each card opens a fresh, filtered search in a new tab. Every result is a real listing page on that platform. Scan the description for the language you're looking for — if it's there, the seller is open.

**9. Reach out.**
Contact the listing agent or seller directly and ask about the terms. That's where the deal starts.
